# CIID Common Language Glossary

System integration work often brings together individuals from a variety of backgrounds. In order to help bridge the gap between these different worlds, CIID has compiled a glossary containing Individuals with Disability Education Act (IDEA), ED Facts, and data management terms to assist in communication and to facilitate understanding during this work. CIID has endeavored to express these terms in language appropriate for a broad audience while balancing the necessary nuances of some of the more technical language. CIID TA Specialists are available to provide additional support and clarification throughout the collaborative process.
